TrueMile EV

The EV logbook that learns your vehicle. It connects to your car's OBD adapter and records every drive and charge on its own — then tells you what they actually cost and how far you can actually go.

What it does

Every drive, logged by itself

Pair a Bluetooth OBD adapter once. From then on the app notices when you drive, records the route, the energy, the temperatures and the speed, and files it — you do nothing.

True cost per mile

Costs follow the energy you actually paid for, charge by charge, at the price you actually paid. Not an average, not a guess.

Your own charging curve

Every DC session is recorded power-by-power. The planner uses your curve and your car's behaviour, not a manufacturer's chart.

Real range, not lab range

Range comes from what your vehicle does in the weather you drive in, at the speeds you drive, with the load you carry.

Battery health you can check

Usable capacity measured from your own charging sessions, tracked over time, with the quality of each measurement shown.

Trip planning that adds up

Plan a route and see where you would stop, how long for, and what charge you would arrive with — from your data, not a generic model.

How it works

  1. Pair an adapterAny Bluetooth ELM327-style OBD adapter. It stays in the car; the app finds it when you get in.
  2. Drive normallyTrips and charges record themselves in the background. Nothing to start, nothing to stop.
  3. Read the truthCost per mile, efficiency, charging curve, battery health, and reports you can export or hand to an accountant.
