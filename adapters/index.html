OBD adapters

TrueMile EV reads your car through a Bluetooth OBD2 adapter — the small dongle that plugs into the port under the dashboard. It stays in the car, and the app finds it when you get in.

Verified

Vgate vLinker MC+The adapter we develop and test on. Bluetooth 3.0 for Android and BLE 4.0 for iPhone, the ELM327 / ELM329 / STN command set, plus MS-CAN and SW-CAN for Ford and GM networks.

Other adapters

Most ELM327-style adapters should workThe app speaks the standard ELM327 command set, so ordinary Bluetooth adapters connect. The vLinker MC+ is the only one we have verified ourselves. Very cheap clones vary: the slow ones return fewer readings per second, and some drop data.
Wi-Fi adapters are not supportedThe app connects over Bluetooth.
What your car reports is up to your carTrips, energy and charging come from standard OBD2 data. Extras such as battery state of health depend on what your particular vehicle exposes.

Buying one for the iPhone app

Choose Bluetooth Low EnergyAn iPhone can only talk to a BLE adapter, or to a classic-Bluetooth one certified under Apple’s MFi program. The vLinker MC+ is dual mode, so a single adapter covers both phones. The iPhone app is still in development.
